## Codebase Patterns
- Backend schemas in `backend/app/schemas.py`, routes in `backend/app/routes.py`, download logic in `backend/app/download_service.py`
- yt-dlp runs in ThreadPoolExecutor (not async-native), use `loop.run_in_executor(executor, ...)` for async endpoints
- Pydantic v2 with `field_validator` and `model_validate`
- Routes use slowapi rate limiter and async SQLAlchemy sessions
- VideoQuality enum in models.py: Q_360P, Q_480P, Q_720P, Q_1080P, MP3
- DownloadRequest has `quality: VideoQuality` (default Q_480P) and deprecated `output_format`
- `get_yt_dlp_options` now accepts `quality: VideoQuality` — video uses height-based format + `-c copy`, MP3 uses FFmpegExtractAudio
- `start_download` and `_run_download` accept `quality: VideoQuality` (not `output_format`)

## 2026-03-13 - US-003
- Replaced `get_yt_dlp_options` to accept `VideoQuality` instead of `OutputFormat`
- Video qualities use height-based format: `bestvideo[height<=N][vcodec^=avc1]+bestaudio[acodec^=mp4a]/bestvideo[height<=N]+bestaudio/best[height<=N]`
- MP3 uses `bestaudio/best` with FFmpegExtractAudio (mp3, quality 320)
- Video uses FFmpegCopyStream with `-c copy -movflags +faststart` (no re-encoding by default)
- Updated `_run_download` and `start_download` signatures to use `quality: VideoQuality`
- Updated `routes.py` to pass quality, with backward compat mapping from deprecated `output_format`
- Files changed: backend/app/download_service.py, backend/app/routes.py
- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - Old OutputFormat enum branches (webm, m4a, wav, flac, ogg) were removed since quality-based flow only supports mp4 video or mp3 audio
  - Backward compat in routes maps audio output_formats to MP3, video to default quality
  - `-c copy` avoids re-encoding when source is already avc1+mp4a compatible (relates to US-004)
